**Challenge — Matchstick Shape Challenge**

- *Act:* Spot shapes in rangoli activity. Direct students to the rangoli on page 15. Ask them to identify and mentally "colour" the triangles, circles, squares, and rectangles as instructed in the book.

**Explore**
